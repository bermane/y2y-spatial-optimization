## Slide 1 - Alberta: where Y2Y's values agree - and what that means with a third already protected

- 12 value positions (6 scenarios x 2 refugia futures) x 51 near-optimal plans each, on the Alberta portion of the Y2Y region (85,133 km2)
- Existing parks are locked in: 27,972 km2 = 33% of the extent - above the 30x30 areal target - and already bank 24-71% of every value (23/27 ecosystem groups present)
- Additions budget 10,083 km2 (the Y2Y-wide fill rate); unprotected land is 87% crown; the private-ranchland (OECM) pool is ~5,393 km2
- Within 5% of optimal, essentially any unprotected cell can be part of a good plan - so the maps use the 2% band
- Tiers = reliability, not a fence

> Context. Methods: spec v0.4.x mirror of the Y2Y-wide study plan v0.14.1; results_log R1-R8.
