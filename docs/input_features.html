Human footprint & intactness

human_modificationLandscape intactness

Theobald gHM v3 cumulative human footprint (settlement, agriculture, transport, energy, mining), scaled 0–1. Used inverted as intactness (1 − gHM) so higher = more natural. The project pulls a Y2Y-clipped Earth Engine asset (v202606).

Notes

Orientation

Two layers are re-oriented so higher = more conservation value: human modification → intactness, and climatic velocity → refugia. All others are used with higher already meaning “more.”

Grid

Every layer is reprojected to ESRI:102008 (North America Albers) and resampled to the analysis grid — 1 km native; current model runs aggregate to 2 km.

‡ EFG encoding

The IUCN source encodes 1 = major, 2 = minor occurrence (verified against the IUCN GET Earth Engine catalog). The pipeline swaps to major = 2 / minor = 1 so major occurrences carry the higher weight.

† Carbon

The irrecoverable-carbon layers are a Berman & McDowell derivation; the foundational global product is Noon et al. 2022 (Zenodo). Confirm the exact source before external use.
